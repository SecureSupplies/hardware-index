What This Board Is (Plain Language) The PC-9XC Variable Gate Card is a pulse permission controller.

It does NOT: Create resonance Generate high power Drive coils Switch current It DOES: Decide when pulses are allowed through Gate pulse trains Sequence outputs Control dwell and spacing Think of it as a digital valve, not a pump. 3.

Where It Fits in the Stanley Meyer System (Critical Answer) ❌ NOT between cell capacitor and chokes ❌ NOT between transformer and chokes ✅ YES — between PWM / frequency control and power switching Correct placement: PLL / Frequency Generator ↓ PC-9XC Variable Gate Card ← (THIS BOARD) ↓ MOSFET / BJT Power Switch ↓ Transformer Primary ↓ Chokes ↓ Cell (Capacitive Load) 👉 It gates the control signal, not the energy path. 4.
