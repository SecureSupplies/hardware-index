Meyer WFC 11 Cell Lower Base is another integral part of the Water Fuel Cell (WFC) setup, specifically designed to support the lower portion of the cell assembly while also playing a key role in managing and protecting components involved in the fuel cycle and wiring.

This 3D-printed lower base holds multiple functions, essential for the cycling of nano bubble water fuel to and from the cell.

Here’s how it works in Meyer’s system: Fuel Cycling Support: This lower base includes specific sections for channeling or guiding nano bubble water fuel to the fuel injection rail.

As the hydrogen and oxygen nano bubbles are generated within the cell, they need to be continuously cycled out to maintain optimal pressure and flow.

The lower base facilitates this cycling by providing a structured pathway, ensuring the generated fuel is efficiently moved towards the injection rail where it can be utilized in the engine or energy-generating apparatus.

Protection for Wiring: The lower base also includes features to cover and protect the electrical wiring that powers and controls the WFC.

Because the WFC setup involves high voltage and electrolysis, the wiring must be shielded from the wet environment within the cell to prevent short circuits and potential hazards.
