Meyer Nano-Bubble Water Fuel Tank Steam Defrost Resonator Technical Description Join Us The Stanley A.

Meyer Nano-Bubble Water Fuel Tank Steam Defrost Resonator was a specialized auxiliary device designed to prevent freezing of nano-bubble-conditioned water in cold environments without heating the bulk water mass.

Unlike conventional heaters, this system was not intended to raise water temperature.

Maintaining water below 24 °C was critical, as higher temperatures cause nano-bubbles to coalesce, escape suspension, and lose their electro-chemical activity.

The resonator therefore operated in a non-thermal, phase-boundary control mode rather than a heating mode.

Functional Purpose Primary Function: Prevent freezing of nano-bubble water in sub-zero conditions while preserving nano-bubble suspension integrity.

Secondary Function: Enable cold-weather operation of water fuel systems where freezing would otherwise disable or destroy the fuel medium.

Operating Principle The device generated localized steam and vapor excitation at boundary surfaces (tank walls, fluid interfaces, or internal resonant structures) using resonant energy input, not bulk heating.
