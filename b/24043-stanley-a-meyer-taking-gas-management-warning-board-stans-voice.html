Stanley A Meyer Taking Gas Management Warning Board Stans Voice Who Wants to Clone Stans Voice and Make him Speak warnings and comments and status of the GMS ?

High Temp , Shut down , check your Nano Bubble Water Fuel Level in Cell, Warning high pressure, Watch Rpm speedy here is a break outboard and sound card , Key point The Audio Shield doesn’t “talk” or display messages on its own.

It’s for audio input/output (record/play tones, measure signals).

To “say things” or show warnings, you use: Teensy + code to detect conditions (temp high, fault, etc.) Then output the warning via one of these channels: Touchscreen display (best) Buzzer / speaker / audio output (audio shield helps here) PLC / HMI via industrial comms (advanced but doable) Option A (fastest): Touchscreen warnings (recommended) What you need A TFT touchscreen (SPI or parallel) Teensy 4.1 Temp sensor (DS18B20 / thermistor / TMP36 / MAX31855 if high temp) How it works Read temperature continuously If temp > threshold: show big red warning screen latch the fault optionally shut down outputs Example behaviors “TEMP HIGH — SHUTDOWN” “OVER CURRENT — RESET REQUIRED” “LOW WATER — CHECK SYSTEM” This is the cleanest “PLC-like” experience on a Teensy.
