Meyer–style U-Core Bobbin designed specifically for 8XA VIC transformer configurations used in advanced water fuel cell and electrolysis research.

The bobbin is engineered to correctly fit a ferrite U-core (U126×91×20-CF13) and supports dual tube cell systems wired in series, following Meyer-inspired resonance and inductive coupling principles.

This component is intended for serious experimental builders, researchers, and developers working on high-voltage pulsed DC inductive systems.
