THE STACKED CELL WITH RED LED RING Daniel Donatelli Hydrogen Architect Developer : Power Gas Energy Storage Projects October 9, 2024 Stanley Meyer's water fuel cell technology, specifically showing a stacked cell circuit that uses red LEDs to weaken electrons and energize gases in the cell.

The setup you are referring to seems to rely on the following principles: Electron Energizing with LEDs: The red LEDs (likely 640 nm wavelength) are used to excite or energize the gas molecules within the fuel cell.

The weakening of electrons by the LED light helps move them to an outer orbital, making them easier to remove from the atoms.

This step is crucial in preventing the reformation of water molecules after gas separation.

Electron Removal and Charge Retention: By weakening the electrons, the cell promotes a positive charge on the gas molecules, preventing them from recombining into water.

This positive charge assists in maintaining an excited state in the gas, further increasing the energy potential for combustion or fuel use.

In essence, this boosts the overall fuel energy without relying heavily on thermal energy (heat).

Stacked Cell Design: The "stacked cell" design aims to optimize gas production and electron extraction.
